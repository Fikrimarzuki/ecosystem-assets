# M1.0l-A1 Mom head layer extraction and fidelity

**Outcome:** the accepted Mom v2 master's head is split into slot layers using **existing pixels only**. Every master pixel sits in exactly one layer, unchanged. Krita's own composite of those layers reproduces the master with **0 differing visible pixels and 0 alpha differences**.

Separated:

- **eyes:** near and far;
- **far brow;**
- **mouth;**
- **head skin:** face, ear, nose, blush;
- **visible neck.**

Not separated, because no reliable boundary exists in the flat art:

- the near brow, which is painted into the hair sweep;
- the hair as front versus back;
- the face rim.

Nothing was painted, generated, recoloured, resampled or mirrored. Holes that would appear when a part moves or is swapped are **mapped, not filled**.

The layers are **not animation-ready**. Blink and talk need painted frames; head movement needs hair separation, a painted back-hair mass and a neck extension.

Nothing was committed, pushed, deployed or approved. No gameplay or Phaser change. Paths are relative to `projects/momee/`.

## 2. Painting route (documented, not executed)

1. Existing artwork is **extracted without repainting** (this milestone).
2. Missing artwork is **identified explicitly**: the §6 map and the hidden "TO PAINT" placeholders in the Krita file.
3. New artwork comes from **reference-guided image generation** (the route that produced v2: ChatGPT, OpenAI) **or an illustrator**. It is delivered as files with recorded provenance.
4. New artwork must **match accepted Mom v2**, which is the visual authority. v1.2 stays historical identity context.
5. **The owner reviews every new expression** before animation integration.

Krita scripting is not used to paint. M1.0i showed that code-drawn art falls short of the master, and nothing in this pilot depends on it.

The layer PNGs are also kept in `candidates/m1.0l-a1-construction/layers/`. Masks and rules are in `masks.json`:

- **features:** hand-read polygons placed in flat skin;
- **head skin:** colour flood from face and ear seeds, an ear-outline polygon, and enclosed pixels;
- **neck:** colour flood inside a collar-bounded polygon;
- **chin outline:** goes with the head;
- **everything else:** base.

**Draw-order caveat:** the hair is still in `base`, which sits *below* the face layers. That makes no difference in the neutral state, since the layers don't overlap. But until `hair-front` is separated, a replacement near-eye frame must stay inside the `eye-near` mask, whose top edge follows the fringe. Otherwise it would draw over the hair.

## 7. Facial animation specification (no frames generated)

**Blink** (`face-eyes` slot swap; each frame holds both eyes as `eye-near` + `eye-far`):

- **Sequence:** open → half → closed → half → open.
- **Timing:** half 50 ms, closed 70 ms, half 50 ms; random interval 2–6 s; 10 % chance of a double blink after 200 ms.
- **Paint:** `half` and `closed` frames, plus `happy` (closed arcs) later.
  - Each frame fully covers the open-eye footprint with opaque eyelid and skin.
  - Boxes: eye-near [332, 245, 424, 326], eye-far [461, 256, 535, 334].
  - Same lash style, lid crease and warm brown line as v2.

**Talking** (`face-mouth` slot swap, no lip-sync):

- **Sequence:** neutral → talking-a (small open) → talking-b (wider open) → … → neutral.
- **Timing:** about 9 fps (110 ms per frame), with a and b in varied order; close through talking-a (80 ms).
- **Paint:** inside the mouth box [398, 339, 456, 366]. The wide frame may extend down to y ≈ 372. Each frame carries its own skin over the whole neutral footprint.

**Idle** (needs separation that does not exist yet):

- **Breathing:** torso scaleY 1.000 → 1.015 about the feet anchor (≤ 2 %), on a 4 s sine. Needs the torso separated from the head and arms.
- **Head movement:** the head group (head-skin, face-*, hair-front, hair-back) moves +4 master px (1 u, 1 px at @1x) with the breath, lagging 150 ms; or rotates ≤ 1° about the head joint (390, 398). Needs the hair split, a painted back-hair mass, the neck extension and the head-skin rim.
- **Optional hair sway:** hair-back ±0.5° about the head joint, on a 5–6 s cycle out of phase with breathing.

**At @1x**, the eye boxes are about 23×20 px and the mouth about 15×7 px. Frames should be judged at @1x and @2x as well as at master size.

## 8. Facing direction

Mom stays **native left-facing** (D15); nothing was mirrored. For a future rig adapter (not implemented):

- **Flip flag:** add `nativeFacing: "left"` to the rig JSON. Container `scaleX` is +1 to face left and −1 to face right, the inverse of the right-facing Boy.
- **Pivots:** mirror about the anchor axis (x′ = 800 − x on the master canvas; scale with the tier).
- **Eye names:** eyes are named **near/far**, not left/right, so the names stay correct after a flip.
- **Mirroring risks:** a flip moves the side part and the baked lighting. Per D15, flip only when she must face right, and have the owner review a flipped preview first.
