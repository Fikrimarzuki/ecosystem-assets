## 3. Pre-intake: glow trim (derivative only)

The candidate already had a transparent background, so no keying was needed. `candidates/m1.0k-a2-construction/deglow.mjs` changes **alpha only** in a derivative:

- **Glow:** 10,604 pixels with alpha ≤ 4 that lie ≥ 3 px from the solid figure are cleared.
- **Strays:** one detached 15 px fragment is removed.
- **Interior opacity:** 580,561 pixels at alpha ≥ 240 that lie ≥ 4 px inside the figure become 255. Every other pixel is scaled by 255/253, which keeps the 1–4 px anti-aliased falloff.

RGB never changes on kept pixels. Composited over any background, the result differs from the original by at most 13 levels (mean ≤ 0.5). Visually it is lossless. The trim is deterministic: two runs give the same bytes.
