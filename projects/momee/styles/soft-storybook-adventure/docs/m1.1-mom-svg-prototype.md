# M1.1: Mom SVG Visual Prototype (planned, not started)

**Status:** not started. M1.1a (reference registration, [Mom spec](mom-visual-spec-v1.md)) is done, and the first prototype is defined as [M1.1b](m1.1b-mom-head-upper-body-prototype.md): head, hairstyle and upper body. Prepared by M1.0m. It begins only after the master reference image is in `../references/` and registered.

## Goal

Recreate the new reference's Mom, in her neutral pose, as structured, editable SVG, and stop for the owner's visual approval.

## Scope

1. Recreate the neutral pose from the reference, following the *animation-aware faithful recreation* policy (style bible §13).
2. Build the SVG from named, grouped, editable components (candidates: hair back, head and face, hair front, eyes, eyebrows, mouth, torso, arms, hands, legs, feet, accessories).
3. Preserve the reference's character identity: face proportions, hairstyle and silhouette, clothing and recognisable colours.
4. Define potential animation pivots (head joint, neck, shoulders, elbows, wrists, hips, knees, ankles, feet anchor).
5. Produce a visual comparison against the reference.
6. Validate that the SVG opens and edits correctly in Inkscape.
7. **Stop for owner visual approval.**

## Not in scope

- A rig, animation or any Phaser integration.
- Changes to the application, its scenes, camera or gameplay.
- Other characters (Boy, Girl, Dad) or environments.

## Locations

SVG sources in `../characters/mom/source/`, transparent exports in `../characters/mom/exports/`, comparisons in `../characters/mom/review/`.

## Entry conditions

- The reference image exists, is registered, and its provenance is recorded.
- The owner confirms Mom's identity-critical features on the sheet.
