## Candidate findings

**A — Classic Illustrated Storybook.** Closest to original B's approachable illustrated charm. Warm contour drawing, larger bright eyes and soft peach washes read well. The lower face, long neck, collar and shoulders provide adult cues, but the eyes are rounded and the face reads younger than the M1.1f alternatives. Small pointed outer lashes remain despite the avoid instruction. Warm wash texture is clear enlarged and subdued at 144 px. This is a plausible storybook direction with facial-design gaps, not a clean pass.

**B — Cinematic 2D Animation.** Smooth shading, a more developed nose/cheek structure, stronger neck/collar shadows and bright sculptural hair produce the most dimensional result. Little contour drawing remains outside the eyelids. The larger catchlit eyes are alert rather than sleepy. However, the satin hair and rounded lighting push toward a rendered 3D animation look; this weakens the strict 2D requirement. Outer lash points also remain. Broad lighting survives well at 144 px. Distinct direction, with medium and lash reservations.

**C — Handcrafted Gouache.** The strongest departure from the retained smooth paintings. Broad sienna/copper hair masses, matte face planes, opaque coral shapes and directional strokes create a traditional painted picture-book appearance. At 144 px the larger shape grouping survives even though individual bristle marks do not. Eyes are open and warm but quite round; their dark upper edges still have small points. The face also reads younger than the prior adult study. The handling is meaningfully gouache-like rather than merely grain added over smooth shading. Distinct direction, with adult-face and eyelid reservations.

They use the full original with uniform scale and padding. Visible figure crown-to-blouse height and placement are shared. Measured: the figure-normalized sheets remove the misleading effect of M1.1f's larger canvas padding. The original B crop has an opaque cream background and pre-existing clipped hair/shoulder edges; those were preserved, not background-removed. Its face has far fewer source pixels than the new paintings, so magnified softness cannot establish a quality deficit.

These are style-driven differences, so the comparison is not an identical-geometry rendering test. The silhouette margins returned by generation did not follow the requested 8% exactly; transparent padding in presentation copies provides consistent review framing without repainting.
