## 3. Silhouette exception

Mom v2's silhouette overlap with the accepted v1.2 extraction at @1x is **0.884**. The owner accepts this because the difference is **primarily slightly fuller hair and wider trousers** (outline width, same pose).

- **Scope:** recorded as a **character-specific exception for Mom v2 only**, under `silhouetteException` in the manifest and in `character-master-spec.md` §8.2.
- **Global rule:** the ≥ 0.90 guideline for same-pose masters is unchanged, and the check's threshold in `scripts/momee-character-master.mjs` is unchanged.
- **Consequence:** later layered composites should be compared against v2, not v1.2.
