- **Eyes:** slightly smaller, soft brown eyes with two highlights and a small outer lash flick.

- **The figure must be ≥ 1160 px tall** from hair top to soles (for example a 1024×1536 output with the figure at ≥ 1300 px), with nothing cropped.
- sRGB PNG. Keep the original file, and any embedded C2PA, untouched.

## 5. Intake procedure (ready to run)

1. Save the original, untouched, in `candidates/m1.0k-a/` and record its SHA-256 and metadata.
2. Key the background non-destructively with `candidates/m1.0j-construction/extract2.mjs`: crop the whole image, set the background from the border, and list reviewed enclosed gaps as seeds.
3. `node scripts/momee-character-master.mjs place <extracted.png> --char mom --out source/characters/mom/mom-master-v1.png`. This applies one uniform premultiplied area-average **downscale** onto the 800×1248 canvas, with the soles on row 1215 and the feet centred at x 400. It **refuses any enlargement.**
4. `node scripts/momee-character-master.mjs check source/characters/mom/mom-master-v1.png --char mom --chin-y <row>` (exit 1 on any failure). It checks:
   - canvas, transparency and anti-aliasing;
   - no cropping at the edges;
   - figure height 1160 ±1 %, soles on the ground line, feet centred on the anchor;
   - no stray pixels, no baked shadow;
   - head proportion 3.2–3.5;
   - left facing;
   - silhouette IoU and 10-band CIEDE2000 palette against the accepted extraction;
   - a detail residual.
5. Create the comparison sheet at matched character height and the bedroom previews with `candidates/m1.0j-c-construction/scale_renders.mjs` (290 u at `MOM_SPOT`; downscale only), plus a Krita working file in the M1.0j-B pattern (original, cleanup layer, masks, reference and guides).
6. Register the entry with `visualAcceptance: null`, `productionApproval: null`, rights `unverified`, and stop for your visual review.
