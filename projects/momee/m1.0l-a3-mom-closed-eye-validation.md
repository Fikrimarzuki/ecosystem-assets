## 5. Extraction feasibility and composite results

**Resolution.** Panel 3 shows the head at about **×1.23** the master's scale, so it reaches the master by **downscaling ×0.81**. No enlargement is needed; this passes, unlike M1.0l-A2.

**Composite test.** The A1 masks were filled from the downscaled, aligned panel 3 on a **copy** of the master; nothing else was changed.

| Measure | Near eye | Far eye |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pixels changed outside the masks | 0 (by construction) | 0 |
| Colour step at the mask edge | **14.3**, against v2's own 8.1 | **15.9**, against v2's own 2.2 |
| Edge pairs with a step over 25 levels | 44 of 350 | 24 of 308 |
| Visible result | A lighter patch outlines the mask; the blush is cut | A lighter patch outlines the mask |

Panel 3 also differs from the master by 12.0 on average outside the eyes, so the surroundings were redrawn. Any cut from it brings mismatched skin with it.

Fixing this would mean recolouring, feathering or repainting the skin, which this milestone rules out. **The file is therefore reference only.** No candidate layers were registered, and the failed composite is kept only as review evidence (`review/m1.0l-a3/closed-eye-composite-test.failed.head-1x.png`).

Both eyes were checked separately (the table above): both fail on skin and seams, and both pass on design, fringe and contour.

## 9. Recommendation

**Needs revision.** Keep this closed-eye design and re-deliver it as a seam-free edit:

1. **Ask for an edit of the v2 head image itself** (same file in, same framing out), not a documentation sheet. No labels, panels, checkerboards or claims.
2. **Change only the eye areas.** Skin, blush, hair, fringe and cheek outline must keep v2's exact colours; this version repaints the skin lighter and yellower.
3. **Keep the design shown in panel 3.**
4. **Deliver at master scale or larger** (this one was ×1.23, which is fine), as a plain PNG. The intake will only downscale.
5. **Acceptance at intake:** composite with the A1 eye masks. The colour step at the mask edge should be within about 2× v2's own edge contrast and the skin within a few levels; then owner review.
6. **Fallback:** if the image tool can't keep the skin identical, an illustrator paint-over of these closed eyes on the v2 Krita file is the reliable route.
