## Component table

**Extract** means the pixels already exist and can be isolated by masking without repainting, though a seam or hole may be left behind once the part is lifted out. **Paint** means the pixels do not exist in the artwork and need new illustration in v2's style.

| Component (slot) | From existing v2 art | Needs new painting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head and neck overlap (`head-skin`, neck) | **Extract:** the visible face and ear. | **Paint:** the neck continuing up under the chin and down under the collar; the skull and cheek hidden behind the front hair and ear. |
| Eyes and eyelids (`face-eyes`) | **Extract:** the open eyes as the `open` attachment, with iris, highlights and lashes. | **Paint:** the skin under each eye attachment. `half`, `closed` and `happy` (closed arcs) are all new; so is the eyelid skin they sit on. |
| Brows (`face-brows`) | **Extract:** the neutral brows. | **Paint:** `raised`. Skin under the brows (hair currently covers part of the near brow). |
| Mouth expressions (`face-mouth`) | **Extract:** the closed smile as `neutral`. | **Paint:** `happy` (open smile), `talking-a`, `talking-b`, and the skin under the mouth. |
| Blush (`face-blush`) | **Extract:** the blush pixels, if they can be separated from the skin tone. | **Paint:** skin underneath once it is lifted. |
| Front hair (`hair-front`) | **Extract:** the fringe, the side sweep, and the locks in front of the shoulders and chest. | **Paint:** the forehead and shoulder areas the locks cover. |
| Back hair (`hair-back`) | **Extract:** only the thin edge of long hair visible beside the head and body. | **Paint:** almost all of it: the long-hair mass behind the head and body, which no pixel shows. |
| Torso (`outfit-top`, `body-skin`) | **Extract:** the visible blouse with collar, buttons and rolled sleeves. | **Paint:** the blouse behind the arms and front locks; the body skin under the collar; the waist where the blouse meets the trousers. |
| Arms and hands (`arm-*`, `hand-*`) | **Extract:** the clasped hands as one `hand-front/clasped` attachment, and the visible forearms. | **Paint:** upper and lower arm split with an elbow overlap; wrists; the far arm; **unclasped** hands and fingers. Clasped hands hide most of each other. |
| Shoulder overlaps | Nothing to extract; the shoulders sit under the sleeves and hair. | **Paint:** shoulder caps under the rolled sleeves, and the hair-covered shoulder line. |
| Legs and feet (`leg-*`, `shoe-*`, `outfit-bottom`) | **Extract:** the visible trouser legs, hems and shoes. | **Paint:** thighs and hips under the blouse hem and waistband; ankles and shoe tops under the hems; the hidden part of the back leg and shoe where the legs overlap. The trousers must be separable so the legs can swing. |
| Shadow | Nothing: none is baked in. | A new contact shadow on the `shadow` layer. |

Summary: **the visible state of every part can be extracted**, but each lifted part leaves a hole, and everything behind or under it is new painting.

## Blockers

1. **Production approval is pending.** Animation work is only a candidate until the owner approves.
2. **Rights are unverified.** The project's rights review for ChatGPT/OpenAI-generated art is not done. This blocks any `Approved` status.
3. **The generator's model identifier, prompt and timestamp are unknown.** Newly painted parts cannot be regenerated to match without a reference-guided workflow; keeping them in v2's style needs an illustrator or the same kind of tool, with recorded provenance.
4. **No tool in the coding session paints new art** (M1.0k-A finding). The painting has to come from the owner, an illustrator or the owner's image tool, delivered as files.
5. **Facing contract:** Mom is native left-facing (D15), against the rig's facing-right authoring. The rig milestone must handle flipping.
6. **Silhouette exception (v2 only):** a rebuilt, layered Mom may drift from v1.2 more than the accepted v2 does, so layer composites should be checked against **v2**, not v1.2.

## Recommended M1.0l-A scope

**Plan and pilot the layer work only; no rig, no gameplay.**

1. **Decide the painting route** (illustrator, image tool, or paint-over) and record it.
2. **Write the part sheet:** a per-slot checklist with masks, overlap targets and canvas coordinates on the 800×1248 master, using the table above.
3. **Pilot the head only:** extract the visible face, eyes, brows, mouth and front hair into slot layers in a copy of the working Krita file, and list exactly what is missing. Optionally commission the missing head parts: neck, eyelids, `half`/`closed`/`happy` eyes, `raised` brow, three mouth shapes.
4. **Check:** composite the pilot head back and confirm it equals the v2 master pixel for pixel, plus the spec §8.5 test (identical alpha outside the face slots).
5. **Stop for owner review.**

Defer arms, hair-back, torso and legs to M1.0l-B onward, since they depend on the painting route. The Boy follows the same pipeline later.
