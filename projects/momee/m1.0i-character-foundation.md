## 4. Existing Boy candidate versus the master

The M0.3c.1 ¾ candidate (`candidate.boy-3quarter-v1` and its refined cutout) is **not approved**. Its known differences, confirmed by eye on the fidelity sheet:

- **Hair:** warmer, lighter chocolate with reddish highlights and fine strand detail; the master's is near-black with tan sheen and chunkier locks.
- **Eyes:** brown irises with visible whites and a smaller eye; the master's are larger and near-black, with less white.
- **Brows and mouth:** a thicker far brow; a closed smile where the master's hero is open-mouthed.
- **Outlines:** at the 200 u gameplay height its lines are ≈ 0.6 u (the master's small figures ≈ 1.4 u); the hair outline is near-black (≈ 11:1 against skin).
- **Proportion:** 2.51 heads; the master's ¾ view is ≈ 10% more head-heavy.

Even so, at gameplay size (68–94 CSS px head) it is **far closer to the master than the M1.0i probe** (fidelity sheet, row 3).

## 5. The fidelity probe and the assessment

**What it is.** The Boy's front head at 6 px per world unit, built from hand-specified Bézier shapes in Krita. Parts live in groups (`hair-back`, `ears`, `head-skin` with clipped shade and blush, `face-eyes`, `face-brows`, `face-nose`, `face-mouth`, `hair-front` with clipped shade, sheen and strands, and line layers). Expressions are swapped layers named by slot/attachment: `face-eyes/{open,happy}`, `face-brows/{neutral,raised}`, `face-mouth/{neutral,happy,talking}`. Colours are sampled from the master. Two refinement passes were made (eyes, hair silhouette).

**What works.** Expressions share one head, so identity cannot drift: all three renders have identical alpha bounds and differ only inside x ±25, y 39–77 head units. Eye placement (≈ 67% down the head), blush, nose and mouth sizes follow the spec; slot names follow the pipeline.

**What does not.**
1. **Hair:** a stiff, spiky silhouette in a different cartoon idiom; the master's soft, sweeping, overlapping locks and tan sheen are not reproduced.
2. **Eyes:** closer after pass 2 but generic; the master's glossy depth, lash weight and highlight shapes read richer.
3. **Rendering:** flat vector fills with blurred shading; the master is softly painted, with colour-varied, pressure-varied lines.
4. **At gameplay size** (68 and 94 px heads) the probe still reads as a different boy, mainly because of the hair silhouette.
5. **Defect:** the tallest crown tuft touches the canvas top (≈ 1 u clipped; 27 edge pixels).

**Conclusion.** Code-driven QPainter drawing through the Krita bridge can produce clean, layered, consistent parts, but not character art at the master's fidelity. More iterations would tune parameters, not close the idiom gap. Expansion stopped here.

## 6. Rig readiness

The probe is **not animation-ready**. It has no body; the face layers are full-canvas (not trimmed or packed); there are no pivots, no `anchor-head-top`, no overlap painting under the hair; the canvas is not on the runtime tier grid (`@1x` = 1 px/u); and there are no closed or half eye frames. Any future character source should follow asset-pipeline §4: authored facing right, ¾ view first, feet origin, `slot/attachment` layer names, 4–8 px painted overlap at joints, separate `hair-back`/`hair-front`, and expression frames on one shared head.

## 7. Source and export workflow (as used, and proposed)

Used: Krita 5.3.4.1 with the krita_mcp 1.0.0 bridge (Python 3.13.5, PyQt5 5.15.11). Construction scripts are in `candidates/m1.0i-construction/` (with `m1.0h-construction/kpaint2.py`, unchanged). The review renders are transparent PNG, sRGB and unprofiled. There are no runtime exports, so nothing has a `runtimeDestination`.

Proposed for a real character source (once a route is chosen): a master at ≥ 2.5 texels/u (≈ 4 px/u, Boy ≈ 800 px tall, Mom ≈ 1160 px) with one canvas per character and the feet anchor on a fixed ground line; tiers `@1x` = 1 px/u and `@2x` = 2 px/u by uniform downscale; trimmed `slot/attachment` part PNGs; pages ≤ 2048²; every file hashed into this manifest.

## 8. Provenance and rights

No external artwork or image-generation model was used in M1.0i. Reference excerpts on the sheet are pixel copies drawn scaled; the master stays byte-identical (MD5 `27a2b706…`). All entries are `Candidate`, rights **unverified**, approval `null`. The master's own rights remain unverified (D9).

## 9. Owner decisions

1. **Character source route** (the main decision):
   - **A. Illustrator-painted** Boy and Mom masters in Krita, following §3, with Claude handling separation, naming, exports, manifest and validation. Highest fidelity and the clearest rights.
   - **B. Owner's image-generation tool** from the master (the M0.3c/M0.6b route), with prompts and C2PA logged, then Krita clean-up, separation and derived expression layers. Faster; rights need D9 evidence.
   - **C. Paint-over of the existing Boy candidate** for expressions (eye and mouth layers) at ¾. Quick and on-model at game size, but it inherits the candidate's eye and hair deviations, and gives no front view and no Mom.
   - Not recommended: continuing procedural Krita drawing for characters.
2. Confirm the talking-frame definition (open eyes and an open mouth) or keep the master's closed-eye talking frame.
3. Whether the Boy's next source should correct the candidate's deviations (§4) toward the master.
4. Height line-up approval (D2), and rights verification (D9) for any lineage.
5. Whether to keep the probe as a layer-structure reference or discard it.

## B4. Bedroom integration study

The M1.0h room (layout unchanged) with the v1 Boy (¾ neutral) at `CHILD_START (520, 630)` and Mom (¾ neutral, **mirrored** to face him, per the rig contract's "facing left = flip") at `MOM_SPOT (840, 560)`, at correct relative scale, with two-stage contact shadows. Study-only clipped layers add a warm window rim (screen) on the window side and a soft falloff (multiply) away from it. The review sheet shows it beside the M1.0h Boy-candidate + Mom-placeholder composition, with mobile crops at real CSS size: 390×844 child-and-mom (1.17), 844×390 overview (0.85) and 390×844 bed (0.94).

**Findings:**
1. Both characters read at phone size, and Mom's taller silhouette makes the 1.45× relationship clear.
2. The child-and-mom frame shows only Mom here, because in that beat the child walks to her approach point `(730, 590)`, while the study keeps him at `CHILD_START`.
3. **Lighting conflict:** baked upper-left light is wrong for anyone mirrored or standing left of the window. Mirroring Mom flips her key to the upper right, away from the window. Final characters should bake only soft, near-frontal light and get window-relative rim light at runtime (or per-side variants).
4. The flat-vector characters look slightly pasted onto the softer painted room (no texture, crisper edges); the room and the characters still need a shared rendering pass.

## B7. Source and export workflow (as run)

1. `chars_v1.py` builds each character document: guides, then both views (hair-back, body parts, head parts), with clipped shading softened by Gaussian blur.
2. `show(view, expression)` toggles the view group and the slot/attachment layers.
3. Krita exports 4 px/u renders; `sips -z` produces @2x and @1x.
4. Files are hashed into the manifest with generated entries (one per character, view and expression).

The scripts are in `candidates/m1.0i-construction/` (`chars_v1.py`, `bedroom_chars.py`, `review_sheet.py`, plus the run 1 helpers), with `m1.0h-construction/kpaint2.py` unchanged.
