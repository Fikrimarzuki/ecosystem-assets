The tier canvases and anchors are the same as the M1.0i placeholders', so the candidates drop into the same slots. Exports are transparent RGBA PNG, unprofiled, with no edge contact; the sole row sits exactly on the anchor row. Each tier is one uniform premultiplied area-average downscale (Boy ×0.389 / ×0.778, Mom ×0.564).

**Method** (scripts and parameters in `candidates/m1.0j-construction/`):

1. Crop the main figure.
2. Flatten any sheet alpha over the panel colour.
3. Key with app-momee's tested `keyImage` algorithm: border flood fill at tolerance 12, speck removal, 2 px edge-band unmixing, with unexplained pixels kept opaque.
4. Clear the owner-reviewable enclosed gaps from listed seeds.
5. Separate the ground shadow (flooding only from the outer background, near-neutral warm darkening, bottom band).

During review, two defects were found and fixed:

- A shadow flood that leaked through the trouser slit and removed part of Mom's right leg.
- A paper blotch left between the Boy's crown tufts.

**Integrity:**

- 0 of 73,811 (Boy) and 0 of 69,481 (Mom) opaque figure pixels differ from the sheet.
- Recompositing extraction, shadow and panel colour reproduces the crop within a mean of 0.95 / 0.38 levels.
- Every difference above 8 levels lies in cleared background (paper tone or faint shadow), never in figure pixels.

**Schema gap.** The schema has one `status` (Working / Candidate / Approved) and one `approval`; it cannot express "visually accepted but not production-approved".

- **What I did:** added the additive `visualAcceptance` and `productionApproval` fields without changing what `status` or `approval` mean. The verifier ignores the new fields.
- **What it would need:** a formal schema field and a verifier rule (for example, `visualAcceptance` must never imply `Approved`).

Rights stay **unverified** everywhere. Provenance is in `candidates/m1.0j/*.provenance.json`, `candidates/characters/{boy,mom}/*-accepted-extract-v1.provenance.json` and `candidates/studies/*accepted*.provenance.json`.

## 13. Recommended next production step

**Obtain clean standalone character illustrations, or have an illustrator repaint the main figures, at master resolution.** The target is one figure per file, ¾ facing the scene, on a flat or transparent background, at **≥ 2.5 px/u**: Boy ≥ 500 px tall (met), **Mom ≥ 725 px**.

Hidden regions should be painted as separate Krita layers using the asset-pipeline §4 slots: neck extension, shoulder caps, `hair-back`, Mom's forearms and hands unclasped (or a dedicated "clasped" pose layer), and leg/trouser overlap. Expression frames (eyes open/half/closed/happy, mouth neutral/happy/talking) should be painted on the *same* head as these accepted figures.

The M1.0j-B extraction and tier pipeline (`candidates/m1.0j-construction/`) can then process those files unchanged. Until then, the stage-2 extractions are the trustworthy, non-destructive interim assets.
