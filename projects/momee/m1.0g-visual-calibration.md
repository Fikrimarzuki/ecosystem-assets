## 2. Bedroom composition study

**Layout.** The study is drawn in world units at 2 px/u directly over the existing `bedroom-layout.ts` positions, so every art element sits where the code already expects it: bed in `x 70–470`, pillow at the fluffed pose centre `(170, 463)`, blanket over `240–470`, door at `1000–1160 × 190–480`, Boy at `CHILD_START (520, 630)`, Mom at `MOM_SPOT (840, 560)`. The `annotated` render overlays the interactable bounds, floor band and camera `mustSee` rectangles from the code. Only the headboard top (≈ 22 u above the bed bounds) and the footboard (≈ 16 u to the right) extend past the existing tap areas.

**Decisions.**
- The window is the light source, placed left of centre (`x 496–704`) so that Mom, at `x ≈ 786–886`, reads against calm wall instead of the curtain. The first pass put the window at `556–764`, and her head overlapped the curtain.
- Warm key light from the upper right of the bed, matching the concept boards' side window: highlights on upper-right edges, shade on lower-left faces, a soft sun patch on the floor and two faint light shafts.
- Gentle one-point perspective (vanishing point `(680, 60)`, depth meets it at 800 u). Floor seams stay horizontal, furniture shows top and front faces, and verticals never converge. Characters remain feet-origin and y-sorted.
- Three readable planes (bible §6): wall and window, furniture, then characters and floor. Wall above the bed and right of the window is left calm for dialogue UI.
- Restrained, grouped decor: two framed pictures (no text), a low toy shelf with books, a ball and a teddy bear, a sill plant, a floor plant by the door and a blush oval rug that grounds the child. Clutter is grouped, never scattered.
- Honey-wood bed with an arched headboard, cream sheet and pillow with cool shading, and a soft sky-mist duvet with a turned-down cuff, as on the concept boards.

**Layers.** Every object is a group of `— base`, inherit-alpha `— soft shade (clipped)`, `— warm light (clipped)` and `— line` layers (28 clipped layers; Krita stores them as `channelflags="1110"`). Top-level groups: annotations (hidden), characters (position study), paper grain (pass-through soft-light and overlay), window light (pass-through screen), bed, decor, door, window and room shell. The placed unmade pillow is a hidden alternate layer.

**Characters.** The Boy is the existing refined cutout, unmodified, scaled uniformly to 200 u. Mom is a flat dashed silhouette labelled *MOM — PLACEHOLDER (not art)*, 290 u, about 3.1 heads.

## 3. Palette and shading rules (proposed)

These are proposals for owner review. They interpret the bible and do not change it.

- **Room palette:** wall cream `#f9eddc` (warmer `#f1dfc8` toward the top corners), honey wood `#e8c69e` (shade `#c99f76`, light `#f5dfbd`), floor `#efd8b6` → `#e2c299`, duvet sky mist `#9dbbc8` (shade `#7393ab`), sheet and pillow cream `#fbf3e6` with cool shade `#c9d2de`, curtains blush `#f3dccd`, plants sage `#9eb49f`. Everything stays within the master's swatches and their mixes. No pure black or white.
- **Line:** warm and darker than the local fill, never black. Lines are coloured per material: wood `#a57b5a`, cream fabric `#a28c80`, duvet `#5f7489`, plant `#667d67`. In world units: interactables **1.25 u**, furniture **1.0 u**, wall decor **0.75 u**, the distant window view no line.
- **Shading:** one warm key light from the upper right for every object, one soft cool shade and one warm highlight per material, blurred edges, gradients allowed, no cel bands. Backgrounds keep lower contrast; interactables (bed, pillow, duvet, door) keep the clearest shapes.
- **Texture:** a very light pass-through paper grain (soft-light mottling plus fine overlay tooth) supplies the "storybook digital painting" feel without heavy texture.

## 5. Character scale findings

The figures below come from the real code: `frameZoom()`/`frameCamera()` in `camera-framing.ts` and `bedroom-layout.ts`, both imported read-only.

- **Adult-to-child:** working heights 200 u and 290 u give 1.45×, inside bible D2's 1.4–1.5×. The Boy candidate measures 2.52 heads (≈ 2.5 ✓); the Mom placeholder is drawn at ≈ 3.1 heads. The current Phaser stand-ins are 168 u and ≈ 246 u (1.46×), so moving to the working heights makes both about 19% taller. Mom's bounds (`y 260–570`) already fit 290 u, while `CHILD_EXTENT` (168) will need updating at integration.
- **Bed-to-child:** the bed frame is ≈ 1.9× the child's height, which is roomy and child-sized. The mattress top at 92 u is 46% of the child's height, mid-thigh to hip. About 75–80 u (≈ 38%) would look more natural for climbing in; owner choice.
- **Pillow size:** the Boy's head is ≈ 80 u tall and ≈ 86 u wide with hair, and the pillow is 106 u, ≈ 1.2× the head width. The concept boards show ≈ 1.6–2×. A larger pillow needs a v3 canvas (e.g. 160×112 @1x) and a larger pillow hit area, a layout change.
- **Floor position:** the Boy's soles at `y 630` sit on the rug in the floor band, in front of the bed's front floor line (578), so depth sorting is correct. Mom's feet at `y 560` sit behind him, as intended.
- **Interaction reach:** a child's arm reaches ≈ 60 u. The bed approach point `(470, 620)` is ≈ 315 u from the pillow, so story interactions can't show the child physically fluffing the pillow from there; that would need a pillow-side stand point near `(250, 600)` (future layout change). Mom's ≈ 95 u reach covers a child at her approach `(730, 590)`; standing, her hands hang near the child's chest, so face-to-face beats need the bible's kneel pose.
- **Camera framing (CSS px):** at the 0.85 floor (320×568 bed, 844×390 overview) the child is 170 px tall, Mom 247 px and the pillow target 106×77. In phone portrait the bed view (390×844) shows world `x ≈ 62–478` at 0.94, with the pillow ≈ 100 px. Every viewport keeps the pillow and blanket targets ≥ 64 px. Rendering caps at 2× DPR, so phones need ≈ 1.7–1.9 texels/u: **@2x is the working tier**, and @1x is below need on phones.

None of this is character approval. The D2 height line-up (`ref.height-lineup-v1`) is still in review.

## 7. Tool notes and limitations

- **Krita 5.3.4.1 + krita_mcp 1.0.0:** all artwork was authored as QPainter Bézier paths in Krita's Python and written to named paint layers. Soft shading uses Krita's Gaussian blur on inherit-alpha layers. This produces clean, consistent, *procedural* painting, not hand brushwork: it approximates the master's "storybook digital painting" but lacks a human painter's varied brush edges, hair-fine detail and expressive line pressure. Bridge pixel writes bypass Krita's undo history; the layers remain fully editable.
- **Group blend modes:** blend modes inside an isolated group have nothing beneath them, so the light and grain groups are set to pass-through.
- **File sizes:** the bedroom study KRA is 39.1 MB, the scale study 13.9 MB and the calibration sheet 10.4 MB, all above the README's proposed 10 MiB ordinary-Git threshold. Most of the weight is many full-canvas soft-gradient layers plus grain (≈ 11 MB). No Momee LFS rule exists; an owner storage decision is needed before any commit.
- **@1x tier:** made with `sips -z`, as in M1.0f. It is a plain resample, not a sharpened downscale.
- **Blender** was not needed: a fixed one-point projection gave consistent perspective for a single room.
- **Next.js 16.4.0** printed its known-vulnerability upgrade notice when the dev server started; the retry continued. No upgrade was run, which is outside this milestone.
