# M1.0k-A3 revised Mom master validation

**Outcome:** the revised illustration (`candidates/m1.0k-a/mom-three-quarter-master-candidate-v2.png`) **passes every intake check** at the master, @2x and @1x sizes. It fixes both A2 problems:

- **head count:** 3.42 heads, where v1 measured 3.68;
- **expression:** the gentle closed-mouth smile.

Side by side at matched size, it reads as the same Mom as v1.2.

One spec fidelity guide narrowly misses: silhouette IoU against the accepted figure is **0.884 against ≥ 0.90**. The cause is outline width (fuller hair, slightly wider trousers), not a different pose.

**Recommendation: ACCEPT v2 as the Mom master candidate for owner visual review.** Two things stay open before any production approval:

- the owner's judgement on that near-miss;
- generation provenance, which is still unrecorded.

The master, tiers, a Krita working source, comparison sheets and bedroom previews were produced and registered. Every one has `visualAcceptance: null`, `productionApproval: null` and rights `unverified`.

Nothing was committed, pushed, deployed or approved. Gameplay, Phaser, the camera and the bedroom layout are unchanged. Paths are relative to `projects/momee/`.

## 11. Remaining concerns

1. **Silhouette near-miss (§8.2):** IoU 0.884 vs 0.90. My view is that this is acceptable: it's the same pose redrawn with slightly fuller hair and trousers. But the spec makes it the owner's call.
2. **Provenance:** tool, model, prompt, references, date and terms are unrecorded, so rights can't move beyond `unverified`. This blocks production approval.
3. **Small identity drifts to look at:** blouse a touch lighter (ΔE 3.8); eyes possibly slightly larger; hair fuller on one side.
4. **Resolution headroom:** the figure is only 1195 px against the 1160 px target (×0.968). A future revision should not be cropped smaller.
5. **Not yet a stage-4 rig master:**
   - one flat expression;
   - hands clasped;
   - no slot layers or hidden overlaps (§5–7 of the spec).
6. **Portrait framing:** hair tips touch the 390×844 overview frame. This is a layout observation, unchanged from v1.2.
