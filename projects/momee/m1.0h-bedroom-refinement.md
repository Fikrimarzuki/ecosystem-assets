## Visual changes (M1.0g → M1.0h)

**Colour.** The same cream / peach / honey / sage / sky-mist families with a wider value range and slightly more saturation: wood `#e8c69e` → `#e6b983` (shade `#bf8a5b`, deep `#a5714a`), floor `#efd8b6…#e2c299` → `#efd1a3…#dcb07c` with alternating plank tones, duvet `#9dbbc8` → `#8db3cc` (lit `#bcd6e5`), sage `#9eb49f` → `#92b393`, curtains `#f3dccd` → `#f5d4bf`. No neon; the darkest tones remain warm browns and blue-greys, never black.

**Lighting.** One clear daylight source, the window: a warm radial glow on the wall; the four panes projected onto the floor as a sun patch (mullion gaps included) with faint screen-blend light shafts; a soft-light warm bounce; and window-relative lit and shaded sides. The bed's footboard and headboard faces are lit toward the window, the door's left edge catches light while its right side and wall shadow fall away, and the plant is lit from the left. Contact shadows are stronger and two-stage (broad ambient plus a tight core) under the bed, rail, shelf, teddy, plant, door, pictures, pillow and both characters. The scene stays calm: no hard sun edges and no dramatic contrast.

**Furniture.**
- Bed: the mattress is lowered from 92 u to 80 u (40% of the child) and thickened to 36 u, with a piping seam and a lit rounded edge. A dark gap and legs sit under the rail, the rail gets a lip and groove, the headboard inset carries lit and shaded bevels, and the posts are shaded like cylinders.
- Duvet: a lit-to-shaded top gradient, a highlighted front roll, three soft fold ridges (shadow groove plus lit ridge), a hem underside, a turned-down cuff that casts a shadow, and a cast shadow on the rail.
- Door: recessed, with a lit right jamb, a top/left inner shadow, raised panels with lit and shaded bevels, a shaded knob and a trim shadow on the wall.
- Shelf, window sill and frame: front/top shading, cubby depth, and a window reveal.

**Texture.** The M1.0g full-canvas paper grain (soft light plus overlay over everything, characters included) is removed. Texture is now applied only where a material calls for it: clipped wood-grain streaks on the floor, rail, headboard, footboard, door leaf and shelf, and a light clipped fabric weave on the duvet, curtains, rug and pillow. The wall, characters and interaction silhouettes stay clean.

**Outlines.** Weights drop from 1.25 / 1.0 / 0.75 u to **1.0 / 0.85 / 0.6 u** (interactables / furniture / decor). Line colours are deeper and slightly more saturated, still warm and local (wood `#8e5c38`, duvet `#46617e`, fabric `#9c7d6c`, plant `#53715a`). Each outline is slightly heavier on its shadow side (away from the window), suggesting brush pressure the way the Boy's lines vary.

## Character consistency

The Boy candidate (unmodified) was compared with the room at 2× and at gameplay scale.

- **Outlines:** the room's line-to-fill contrast rises from ≈ 2.3–2.9:1 (M1.0g) to ≈ 2.9–3.5:1, closer to the Boy's coloured lines (shirt ≈ 4.1:1). The room's interactable line, 1.0 u, now sits between the Boy at gameplay height (≈ 0.6 u) and the master's small figures (≈ 1.4 u). The Boy's near-black hair outline (≈ 11:1) is still far heavier than anything in the room. That is consistent with the master, where characters carry the darkest lines, but it keeps him visibly crisper than the furniture.
- **Contrast and saturation:** the room moved toward the Boy. His yellow shirt and blue shorts remain the most saturated colours in the scene, which matches the bible's rule that characters and interactables carry the most contrast. Risk: the yellow shirt against the cream wall is only ≈ 1.18:1 in luminance and relies on hue and outline; it reads clearly against the floor and rug, which is where the floor band keeps him.
- **Shading and lighting:** the room now has a window-relative light direction. The Boy is lit softly from the front, with no strong direction. At gameplay size this reads as compatible; a final character set should take a gentle key from the window side.
- **Detail density:** the room is now closer in finish to the Boy (bevels, seams, folds, grain) without competing with him; the wall and upper room stay calm for dialogue UI.
- **Mom:** still a clearly labelled flat placeholder; no production Mom was made.

## Limitations

- The artwork is still procedural (QPainter paths with blurred clipped shading), not hand brushwork. It approaches the concept boards' soft painted finish but lacks varied brush edges, painterly colour shifts within a shape, and expressive line pressure; the weighted outline only approximates the last.
- The concept boards are richer and more saturated, with deeper golden light; M1.0h moves toward them only "slightly" as instructed.
- The bedroom v2 KRA is 38.2 MB and the comparison KRA 19.0 MB, both above the proposed 10 MiB threshold; there is still no Momee LFS rule.
- The pillow v3 size test changes the canvas; v1/v2 integration notes no longer apply to it unchanged.
