## 6. Approval-state contract (D14)

`scripts/lib/momee-approval.mjs` is used by `scripts/verify-momee.mjs` and tested by `scripts/momee-approval.test.mjs` (9 tests, `pnpm test:momee`). It defines:

| State | Field | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Visual direction acceptance | `visualAcceptance` | The owner accepted or rejected a design direction. It implies nothing about production or rights. |
| Production approval | `productionApproval` (legacy `approval`) | The owner approved shipping a file in a production runtime |
| Rights verification | `rights.status` | `unverified` / `restricted` / `verified` (with who, when and evidence) |
| Workflow stage | `status` | Working / Candidate / Approved |

**Errors the verifier now raises:**

- production approval on a non-Approved entry;
- Approved without all of: production approval, verified rights, an owner-accepted visual direction, and exports;
- a rejected direction carrying production approval;
- `approval` disagreeing with `productionApproval`;
- malformed states or dates;
- verified rights without evidence.

All 40 current entries pass (the historical ones need no change). A scratch-copy negative test confirmed that the full verifier fails on three injected contradictions. The README documents the contract; the bible records it as D14.

## 7. Character master specification

The specification is [character-master-spec.md](character-master-spec.md). In summary:

- **Density and sizes:** 4 px/u masters. Boy 640×896 px with feet anchor (320, 864) and an 800 px figure; Mom 800×1248 px with anchor (400, 1216) and a 1160 px figure. These are the same world boxes and anchors as the current tiers.
- **Views:** ¾ facing right first; front later.
- **Face slots:** `face-eyes/{open,half,closed,happy}`, `face-brows/{neutral,raised}`, `face-mouth/{neutral,happy,talking-a,talking-b}`.
- **Overlaps:** hidden overlaps of ≥ 6 u at the neck, shoulders, back hair, elbows, wrists, hips and ankles.
- **Structure:** asset-pipeline §4 slot groups and `slot/attachment` layers in Krita.
- **Exports:** @1x/@2x by downscale only.
- **Fidelity checks:** silhouette IoU ≥ 0.90 against the accepted extraction; palette ΔE₀₀ ≤ 5; head range; edge and overlap QC; recorded provenance.

**Boy:** the accepted illustration is **sufficient for initial production work** (2.57 px/u ≥ 2.5): static previews now, and a base on which an illustrator paints the hidden overlaps and face attachments. Animation needs those new layers, plus an elbow split and `hair-back`.

**Mom:** needs a **genuinely new standalone illustration** of Mom v1.2 at ≥ 1160 px figure height (minimum 725 px), facing right, with unclasped hands or a separate clasped layer. Resampling the current cutout does not qualify.

## 11. Remaining risks

- **Mom resolution:** Mom remains @1x-only until a new illustration exists. Static use on high-DPI phones will look soft (devices want ≈ 1.7–2 texels/u).
- **Static only:** the cutouts cannot animate. The child-in-bed, wave and nod beats need the master's hidden layers and face attachments.
- **Mom's facing:** the accepted v1.2 pose faces left while the rig contract authors facing right. Flipping mirrors her side part. This is an owner decision.
- **Framing:** at the 390×844 overview the wider real art touches or clips the frame; integration must revisit `CHILD_EXTENT` and the Mom bounds.
- **Approval gates:** rights (D9) stay unverified for every lineage, and the height line-up (D2) is still unapproved. Both gate production.
- **Ambiguous rims:** two possible painted rims were left in place; on very dark scenes they could read as specks.
- **Storage:** large KRAs from M1.0g–h still have no storage rule.
