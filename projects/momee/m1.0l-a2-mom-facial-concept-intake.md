# M1.0l-A2 Mom facial animation artwork intake

**Outcome:** the concept sheet `review/m1.0l-a2/mom-facial-animation-concept-v1.png` is **reference only**. None of its expressions can be extracted for the blink-and-talk pilot:

- **Too small:** they are drawn at ×0.36–0.39 of the v2 master, so using them would mean **×2.6–2.8 enlargement**.
- **Redrawn every time:** each panel repaints the whole face, so a frame's surroundings don't match v2 or the other frames.
- **Off-style eyes:** the eye frames use **black lashes**, which break v2's warm brown line work.

No candidate was extracted. The M1.0l-A1 neutral layers are unchanged. **Blink, talking and the combined preview are all blocked** until dedicated expression artwork exists at master resolution.

Nothing was redrawn, filled, enlarged, committed, pushed, deployed or approved. Mom v2's acceptance is untouched. Paths are relative to `projects/momee/`.

## 5. Extraction feasibility

| Requirement | Result |
| --- | --- |
| No upscaling | **Fails.** Every panel would need ×2.6–2.8 to reach 4 px/u: eyes ×2.70–2.78, mouth ×2.70, brow ×2.56. |
| No invented pixels or repainting | **Fails.** The frames only fit if their redrawn surroundings were repainted to match v2. |
| No change to Mom's appearance | **Fails for the eyes.** Black lashes would change her eye design. |
| No visible seams | **Fails.** The skin, lashes and lid edges around each feature differ from v2, so A1-mask cuts would show seams. |
| Alignment with the A1 slots | **Fails.** Panels are offset 1–4 px at thumbnail scale (4–15 px at master) and drawn at slightly different head scales. |
| Near-eye fringe overlap | Not testable at this resolution. The half and closed near-eye lashes reach up toward the fringe, and a frame there must stay under the fringe edge (A1). |

**Using the frames only at the @1x tier was considered and rejected.** They would only need shrinking (×0.69) there. But it breaks the master-first pipeline (spec §7), @2x would still need ×1.4 enlargement, and the redrawn surroundings and black lashes remain.

**Conclusion:** all nine panels are **reference-only**. No candidate layers were created, and the A1 neutral layers were not replaced.

## 11. Recommended next step (M1.0l-A3)

**Commission dedicated expression artwork at master resolution: one edit per frame, not a concept sheet.**

1. **Edit base.** Give the image tool the accepted v2 head at 4 px/u (master crop x 240–580, y 150–480; 340×330 px), enlarged to about 1024 px **only as the tool's input**. Ask for output of ≥ 1020 px for that crop, so the intake only downscales.
2. **Scope of each edit.** Change **only** the eyes, or only the mouth. Everything else must stay identical.
3. **Frames, in priority order:**
   - `face-eyes/closed`, `face-eyes/half`;
   - `face-mouth/talking-a`, `face-mouth/talking-b`;
   - later: `face-brows/raised`, `happy` eyes and mouth.
4. **Style.** v2's warm dark-brown lashes and lid line (not black), the same iris and highlights, the same skin, blush and upper-left light, and painted eyelid skin.
5. **Coverage.** Each frame covers the A1 footprints:
   - eye-near [332, 245, 424, 326], under the fringe;
   - eye-far [461, 256, 535, 334];
   - mouth [398, 339, 456, 366], extending to y ≈ 372 for the wide frame.
6. **Intake (M1.0l-A3).**
   - Place each frame on the 800×1248 canvas by downscaling only, and align it to v2 by feature registration.
   - Cut it with the A1 masks.
   - Accept it only if the pixels **outside** its mask closely match v2 (a seam check), then owner review.
   - Record the tool, date, prompt and input file for each frame.
7. **This concept sheet.** Keep it as a direction reference: the mouth shapes are a good guide; the black-lash eyes are not.

Hair split, back hair, neck extension, face rim, head movement and breathing stay deferred to M1.0l-B. No rig, no Phaser.
