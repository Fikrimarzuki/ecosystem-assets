## How the clips were made

`scripts/blender/animate_explorer.py`, run on the v3 working `.blend`. Its weights and geometry were verified identical to the approved v3 GLB (maximum weight difference 0.00001).

- **Procedural keyframes.** Each clip is a periodic function of phase, keyed **every frame** with linear interpolation. Frame N equals frame 0 exactly, so the loops are seamless by construction rather than hand-matched.
- **Legs: analytic two-bone IK baked to plain rotation keys.**
  - Planted feet travel backwards along the ground at the clip's constant speed. That is what in-place locomotion needs to stay foot-locked once the game moves the character at that speed.
  - Heel strike pivots on the boot heel; heel-off pivots on the toe joint, with the toe kept flat.
  - Swing paths leave and land at the stance speed.
- **Arms, spine and head: forward kinematics.**
  - Arms are lowered 28–36° from the A-pose and held slightly out, with opposing swing (walk ±17°, run ±34°) and bent run elbows (66–75°).
  - The pelvis yaws and rolls, the chest counter-rotates, and the head is stabilised to stay level.
- **Idle:** feet planted at about ±0.16 m (well inside the A-pose's ±0.27 m), soft knees (10–18°), one breath and one weight shift per loop, and a small head drift.
- The generator **re-measures every joint angle and refuses to export** if a limit is exceeded. It caught the first Run draft (knee 107°, hip 63°), which was reduced before export.
- **What is not in the file:** no constraints, drivers, helper bones, mesh edits or weight edits. Only the 22 existing bones are animated, using TRS channels.

## Visual review at gameplay distance (Observed)

Contact sheets: `characters/explorer/previews/anim/sheet-{Idle,Walk,Run}.png`. Each has rows for textured side, textured three-quarter, textured front and clay side, over 6–8 frames.

- **Idle:** relaxed, legs drawn in, soft knees, arms hanging clear of the thighs. Movement is deliberately subtle.
- **Walk:** clear alternating steps, grounded feet with heel-to-toe roll, opposing arm swing, slight pelvis and torso rotation.
- **Run:** clearly distinct from the walk: bent arms, higher knee drive and back-kick, flight phase, 9° forward lean. It reads as a controlled jog-run.
- Close-ups of the worst Run frame show no visible hood or collar damage.

## Remaining limitations

1. **Movement speed mismatch with the game (integration decision).**
   - The game moves the player at **4 m/s** (walk) and **7 m/s** (sprint). The clips are authored at natural speeds of **1.0 m/s** (Walk) and **2.39 m/s** (Run); a 1-second walk cycle can't cover 4 m without absurd strides.
   - Playing the clips at the current game speeds will slide the feet.
   - Options:
     - **(a)** Lower the game speeds toward the clips.
     - **(b)** Use `Run` for the default 4 m/s at a Babylon `speedRatio` of about 1.6, and author a separate sprint clip later.
     - **(c)** Re-author longer-stride clips (limited by the 0.97 m leg reach and the approved knee and hip limits).
2. **Rest pose is still the A-pose.** Clips are relative to it, which is fine for Babylon. The origin is at the body's centre and the height is 1.898 m against the game's 1.8 m capsule; both are still open from earlier milestones.
3. **The Run is a jog-run, not a sprint.** The approved knee limit (100°) and hip limit (55°) cap the knee drive and back-kick.
4. **Minor contacts:**
   - small hand-to-hip brushes at the walk's back-swing;
   - knee-fold and groin interpenetration at the run's peak;
   - hair resting inside the hood.

   None is visible at gameplay distance.
5. **No secondary motion** (hair or jacket), no finger animation, no transitions or blends between clips. Blending is a Babylon-side concern.
6. **Babylon test was headless and skipped materials.** Rendering with textures in a browser is **Untested** here. Textures and materials are byte-identical to v3, which loaded fine in Blender.

## Recommendation

**Ready for Babylon.js integration.** Decide the speed strategy first (Limitations 1); option (b) is the smallest change if the game's speeds should stay.
