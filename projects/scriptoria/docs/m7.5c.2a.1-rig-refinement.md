# M7.5c.2a.1 — Final Rig Refinement (Explorer, 25k, v2 → v3)

Date: 2026-10-17 · Workspace: `scriptoria-assets` only (`game-scriptoria` untouched) · Blender 5.2.2 LTS CLI · nothing installed · not a git repo; no branches, commits or pushes.

Labels: **Verified** = measured by script or checked in files · **Observed** = seen in renders I inspected · **Assumption** · **Untested**.

## Result

**Ready for animation**, with the elbow limitation noted below. **Recommended rig: `characters/explorer/rigged/explorer-neutral-25k-rigged-v3.glb`.**

- v3 is a weights-only refinement of v2. The skeleton, geometry, UVs, materials and textures are unchanged.
- At third-person gameplay distance v3 is visually identical to v2 in idle, walk, run and elbow poses, with **no regressions** (**Observed**, `previews/rig-v2-vs-v3-body.png`).
- Up close it reduces elbow-cuff clipping (**Verified** metric, **Observed** small visual gain).
- Knee and groin are essentially unchanged: the weight changes were within what weights can achieve.

## What changed (v2 → v3)

`scripts/blender/refine_rig.py` starts from the v2 working `.blend`. Its weights are verified identical to the approved v2 GLB: maximum weight difference 0.0, maximum position difference 0.001 mm. It uses the `.blend` because it holds the welded mesh; smoothing weights on the seam-split GLB could open seams. Of 12,470 vertices, 1,439 changed.

| Task | Change | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Elbow cuff clipping | The upper_arm→forearm transition was about 1 cm wide. It is replaced with a smoothstep blend from −6 to +5 cm across the sleeve cuff (244 / 252 vertices). Measured first: the sleeve is a hollow shell (5–7 cm radius) closing onto the forearm skin (2.5–3.6 cm) in a ring at the joint, so the poke is the forearm knifing through the cuff on the inner side of the bend. | Clipping at 90° elbow **141 → 90** new self-intersections. Upper-arm clipping is gone on the left and reduced on the right. Trade-off: the inner fold now compresses (flipped faces 21 → 40, hidden inside the fold). Visually a modest gain; a sliver of forearm skin still shows under the cuff up close. |
| Knee pinching | Same blend for thigh→shin, −7 to +6 cm (199 / 200 vertices). Only the weight the vertex already gave those two bones is redistributed. | Clipping unchanged (111); visually near-identical to v2. The remaining pinch is linear-skinning volume loss, not a weight seam. |
| Hip/groin | Laplacian weight smoothing (6 × 0.5), **masked** to the crotch and inner thigh (\|x\| ≤ 0.13, z −0.08…0.16, thigh/hips-dominant; 544 vertices). Jacket and trouser weights outside the mask are untouched. | Visually unchanged; flipped faces in the 60° hip-flex test rose 19 → 35. Silhouette preserved: weights don't change the rest shape, and posed silhouettes match v2 (**Observed**). |

**Tried and rejected.** v3b moved the elbow blend up the sleeve (−11 to −1 cm) so the stiff cuff ring would travel with the forearm. It was worse than v3 on clipping (111 vs 90), flipped faces (54 vs 40) and forearm twist, so it was discarded.

How to read the counts:
- The large clipping counts in every **arms-down** pose (idle, stance, twists, about 300–500) are present identically in v2. They come from the hands and fingers touching the thighs when the arms come 40° down from the A-pose. That is a pose/collision matter for animation, not a skinning defect.
- Counts are triangle pairs out of 25,000 triangles; flipped faces are at most 0.4% of triangles in any pose.

## Remaining limitations

1. **Elbow cuff (main one).** Weights can't hold a stiff cuff ring rigid through a 90° bend under linear skinning. A sliver of forearm still shows under the cuff up close; it isn't visible at gameplay distance. **No helper bone was added:** the defect is minor at gameplay distance, and a helper bone would change the skeleton and need its half-rotation baked into every clip, since Babylon has no runtime constraints. If it matters later, add one `elbow_helper` per arm, driven at 50% of the forearm bend.
2. **Knee and elbow volume loss** at more than 90°: inherent to linear skinning (*Assumption*: Babylon uses linear blend skinning). Keeping run clips' knee peak at about 100° or less is fine at distance.
3. **Hands vs thighs** when the arms are fully down. Animations should keep the arms about 35° down rather than 40°, or slightly out from the body.
4. **Hair** is rigid on the head and grazes the hood collar on large head turns. There is no secondary motion.
5. **No twist bones:** wrist and forearm twist are fine to ±60°; beyond that is **Untested**. **No finger bones:** static open hands.
6. **Cosmetic:** the material name is `explorer_neutral.001` in all rig versions since v1, a leftover from Blender renaming during the bake. It is harmless and left unchanged to avoid touching materials.
7. **Open integration decisions** (unchanged from earlier milestones):
   - origin at the body's centre, height 1.898 m vs the game's 1.8 m capsule;
   - 2048² textures vs the planned 1024²;
   - Blender-style bone names (`thigh.L`).
8. No Babylon.js check (out of scope).
