# M7.5c.2a — Rigging Feasibility (Explorer, 25k)

Date: 2026-10-17 · Workspace: `scriptoria-assets` only (`game-scriptoria` untouched) · Blender 5.2.2 LTS CLI, no MCP, nothing installed · Not a git repo; nothing committed.

Labels: **Verified** = measured by script or checked in files · **Observed** = seen in renders I inspected · **Assumption** · **Untested**.

## Verdict

**Feasible. Accept the corrected rig (v2) as the base, with a short list of targeted corrections before final animations. No need to reconsider the mesh.**

- The approved 25k mesh skins cleanly onto a 21-bone game skeleton with automatic weights.
- Idle and walk poses deform well (**Observed**).
- Run-range bends (knee at 100°, elbow at 90°, hip at 55–60°) show the usual linear-blend-skinning pinch plus some clothing creasing. It is visible in close-ups but reads correctly at full-body, third-person distance (**Observed**).
- No joint collapsed and no texture tore. The UVs, materials and textures are unchanged, so any texture "damage" can only come from stretching. It is confined to the joint creases (**Observed**, plus metrics below).

### v1 → v2 (targeted correction)

Testing v1 found one real defect. Heat weighting let the **clavicle bones dominate the whole jacket front panel and hood collar** (z 0.15–0.71), so arm motion dragged the jacket and hip motion tore the hem (**Verified** from weight-reach data; **Observed** in renders). `correct_weights.py` produces v2 with two changes:

1. **Clavicle weight clamp.** Clavicle weight is kept only within 0.11 m of the clavicle segment and above z 0.36 (3,872 vertex weights reassigned to the nearest torso bone). Clavicle reach is now z 0.36–0.59.
2. **Light weight smoothing** across the shoulder/upper-arm, elbow and knee transitions.

## Known issues and targeted corrections (proposed; none applied beyond v2)

1. **Knee and elbow volume loss.** This is inherent to linear skinning, and Babylon uses linear skinning (dual-quaternion skinning isn't an option there; *Assumption*, verify in-engine). Fix with one helper bone per knee/elbow that rotates half-way, or keep run poses at about 90° or less. → targeted correction.
2. **Elbow cuff intersection** above about 60° of flex. Weight the sleeve cuff more to the forearm, or accept it (hard to see at third-person distance). → targeted correction.
3. **Hip/groin creasing** at 55–60° flex. Smooth the thigh/hips weights at the groin and the trouser pockets. → targeted correction.
4. **Wide A-stance.** The legs rest far apart, so idle and walk need the thighs drawn in about 5–8°. *Untested*: test it before authoring clips.
5. **Untested ranges:** spine and torso twist, neck/head turn (the hood/collar is weighted to neck and head), forearm twist (no twist bones, so candy-wrapping is likely beyond ±45°), and hair (rigid on the head; no secondary motion).
6. **No finger bones.** The hands are static open hands, fine for idle/walk/run. A run-ready loose fist would need 1–2 finger bones per hand.
7. **Integration decisions still open** (from M7.5c.1d):
   - origin at the body's centre vs feet;
   - 1.898 m height vs the game's 1.8 m capsule and 1.6 m eye height;
   - 2048² textures vs the planned 1024²;
   - bone names are Blender-style (`thigh.L`), not Mixamo-style. That matters only if library animations are retargeted.
8. Rendering was Blender only; there was no Babylon.js check (out of scope).

## Recommendation

**Accept rig v2 and do one targeted-correction pass (items 1–4) before producing Idle/Walk/Run.** The mesh does not need reconsidering: there are no topology-driven failures, and every problem found traced to weights or skinning method, not to the 25k decimation.
