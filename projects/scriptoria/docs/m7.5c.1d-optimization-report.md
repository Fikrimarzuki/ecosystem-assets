## 5. Optimization method

The pipeline is fully scripted and reproducible. Scripts are in `scripts/blender/`.

**Step 1: decimation** (`optimize_explorer.py`). Non-destructive: one linked duplicate per candidate sharing the imported mesh, each with a modifier stack. The stacks are saved in `optimized/explorer-neutral-optimization.blend`.

1. **Weld (1e-5 m).** Re-joins vertices that the GLB split along UV seams; the source becomes closed and manifold. UVs are per-corner in Blender, so the seams are preserved in the UV data.
2. **Decimate, Collapse, uniform.** The ratio is calibrated until it is within ±1.5% of the target; all three landed exactly on target. Symmetry is off because the wrist device is one-sided.
3. The GLB's custom normals are cleared, so shading comes from each candidate's own geometry.

**Step 2: texture re-bake** (`bake_candidates.py`). This was added after inspection showed the decimate-only results were not acceptable (see §10).

- Cycles, selected-to-active from the **untouched source** onto each candidate's own UVs. Cage extrusion 0.01 m, max ray 0.03 m, margin 64 px.
- Bakes all three maps at the source's 2048² resolution and roles: base color (emit pass, sRGB), metallic-roughness (emit pass, non-color), and normal (tangent space).
- Exported as JPEG quality 92, matching the source's JPEG textures. The material keeps the same node layout and glTF channel mapping as the source.

**What was tried and rejected.** All of this was measured and kept out of the deliverables:

- **Vertex-group protection of the head.** In Blender 5.2 the Decimate vertex group acts as a near-hard mask. Weight 0.8 on the head kept 20.4k of a 23.8k budget on the head and starved the body. Uniform collapse already keeps the head at about 27% of the triangles, close to its 26% share in the source.
- **Transferring the source's custom normals** onto the decimated mesh. No visible difference from recomputed normals. It is kept as an optional `transfer` mode in `optimize_explorer.py` and is off by default.

**Process note.** One comparison misread happened during the work. A downscaled comparison strip made the source face look different from an unmodified round-trip export. A pixel diff showed the two are identical, and every quality judgment below uses full-resolution images or the pixel metrics instead.

**What I saw in the inspected images** (all **Observed**, at full resolution where it matters):

- **Full-body views:** all three baked candidates are practically indistinguishable from the source in front, three-quarter, side and back views. The silhouette, A-pose, hair with cyan streaks, navy and white jacket with its logo, tactical trousers with cyan stripes, boots, fingerless gloves and cyan accents are all preserved.
- **Decimate-only (rejected):** visible texture swimming that grows as the count drops. Even 40k smears the crisp cyan chest line into a blob. At 25k and 20k the mouth flattens into a line, the hood rim becomes sawtoothed and the "V" logo distorts. This is why the bake step was added.
- **Baked 40k:** very close to the source in the face close-up.
- **Baked 25k:** the face keeps the eyes, smile and chest line; the jaw and chin are slightly more angular.
- **Baked 20k:** the face is still clean, but in profile the nose and mouth are blunter. In clay the hand is visibly coarser: finger separation is shallower and the thumb blunter.
- **Wrist device:** shape and cyan panel are preserved in all three; the textured close-ups look nearly identical.
- **Clay faces:** faceting on the cheeks and neck increases at 25k and 20k. Texture and normal-map detail largely hide this in textured renders.

## 11. Known issues

1. **Uniform triangle density.** The candidates inherit the source's lack of edge loops. Shoulders, elbows, knees and especially fingers may deform poorly when skinned, and 20k has the least finger geometry. *Assumption until rigging is tested.*
2. **Origin and scale:** the origin is at the body's center and the character is 1.898 m tall. The game plan assumes feet-origin and about 1.72–1.78 m. Not changed, because orientation and dimensions had to stay as they were.
3. **No emissive map.** The cyan accents are painted, not emitted (inherited from the source).
4. **`doubleSided: true`** is kept from the source; it disables back-face culling unnecessarily on a closed mesh.
5. **Texture budget:** 3 × 2048² RGBA maps is about 67 MB of GPU memory uncompressed with mips (3 × 16.8 MB × 1.33). That is above the `code-fps` plan's planning target (1024² per map).
6. **Triangle budget:** the `code-fps` plan targets 10–15k rendered triangles, with a 20k ceiling, for the player. Only 20k meets that ceiling; 25k and 40k exceed it. These are planning targets, not hard limits.
7. **Stale working `.blend`:** `explorer-neutral-optimization.blend` holds the decimation stacks only, not the bake. The bake is reproducible from `bake_candidates.py`.
8. **Stray files outside the workspace:** an early render run used relative paths and wrote 10 preview PNGs to **`C:\characters\explorer\previews\`**. The script was fixed to use absolute paths. Deleting that folder was blocked by a safety check on drive-root deletions, so **please delete `C:\characters` manually**; it contains only those 10 PNGs from 2026-10-15 20:37.
9. Not inspected: the location of the 5–6 mm maximum-deviation spot; rendering in Babylon.js (out of scope); texture appearance at real mip levels in-engine.

## 12. Recommended candidate

**`explorer-neutral-25k.glb` (decimated and re-baked)** as the base for rigging:

- It is visually near-identical to the source at gameplay distances, and the face keeps its eyes, smile and chest light.
- It keeps noticeably more hand and finger geometry than 20k, which matters for skinning.
- At 1.52 MiB it is 83% smaller than the source.

**Fallback:** 20k, if the `code-fps` 20k planning ceiling is treated as firm. It looks equivalent at full-body distance, but its hands and profile are coarser.

40k is not recommended for the game. It is twice the planning ceiling, and its visual gain over 25k only shows in close-ups the third-person camera won't produce.

**This needs your decision.** 25k means accepting a 5k overshoot of a planning target.
